I am a PhD candidate in the School of Human Ecology at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, specializing in consumer behavior and family economics. My research examines how place-based spatial factors, such as broadband infrastructure and population migration, shape household financial decision-making and access to financial services. Research
Immigration and Financial Inclusion
R&R · Journal of Consumer Policy

This paper investigates how immigration-related factors, including citizenship status, generational status, age at arrival, and ethnic enclave concentration, predict unbanked status and alternative financial service use among U.S. households.
